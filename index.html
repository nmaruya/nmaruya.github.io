Calendar Widget for Windows 11

A small personal widget for the Windows 11 Widgets board that shows your Google Calendar agenda in small, medium and large sizes.

What it does

  • Shows today's events, and the days after, from the Google calendars you choose.
  • Highlights what is next, and shows an "all clear" panel when nothing is scheduled today.
  • Keeps showing your last events if you go offline.
  • Read-only: it can never change your calendar.

Your data
