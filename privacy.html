Calendar Widget: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

Calendar Widget is a personal Windows 11 widget that shows your Google Calendar events on the Widgets board. This page explains what it does with your data. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

What the app accesses

When you sign in, the app asks for one Google permission: read-only access to your Google Calendar (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly). It uses that access to read the list of calendars you can see and the events on the calendars you choose: titles, times, locations and event links. It cannot create, change or delete anything in your calendar.

How the data is used

Your calendar data is used only to display your agenda in the widget on your own computer. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling or any other purpose.

Where the data is stored

Everything stays on your own Windows computer, in your user profile:

  • Your Google sign-in token, encrypted with Windows data protection for your Windows account.
  • A short-lived copy of recent events, also encrypted, so the widget can still show your agenda if you are offline.
  • Your settings (which calendars to show, how many days, time format) and a diagnostic log that does not contain your calendar contents.

The developer runs no server for this app and does not receive, collect or have access to any of this data.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. The only network connections it makes are to Google, to sign you in and to read your calendar. If you tap an event, your browser opens that event on Google Calendar.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Calendar Widget's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • To stop the app from accessing your calendar, remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To delete everything stored on your computer, uninstall the app and delete the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\CalendarWidget.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
